Important: RBI and banks NEVER ask for your OTP, PIN, CVV, or full card details over phone/email/SMS.
Common Frauds
  • KYC update scams via fake links
  • Fake UPI collect requests
  • Phishing emails impersonating banks
  • Screen sharing scams
  • Lottery / prize money frauds
Stay Safe
  • Register on DND to block spam calls
  • Report fraud: 1930 (Cyber Crime Helpline)
  • Report online: cybercrime.gov.in
  • Block lost card immediately via bank helpline
  • Enable SMS/email alerts for all transactions
